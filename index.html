Our history

Half a century of music in Cochin

CAC began in 1974, when the Archbishop of Verapoly, Rev. Dr. Joseph Kalanthara, set out to build an institute devoted to music and the liturgical arts. Working with Rev. Fr. Michael Panaikal and Rev. Fr. Joseph Manakkil, he raised a building just behind the old Kerala Times premises — and Cochin Arts & Communications was born.

The early years centred on composing and recording devotional music, arranged by Varghese Malieckal and tuned by Job & George and Jerry Amaldev. As interest grew, CAC formed a full orchestra and Ganamela troupe, drawing singers and musicians from across the region and touring widely with its stage shows.

In 1980, CAC opened its own professional audio recording studio — a rarity at the time — and its recordings became known for their quality. Decades on, the same spirit continues: when Pope John Paul II visited Kerala, it was CAC that coordinated a 100-voice choir and 30-piece orchestra for the occasion.

Mission & commitments

What we're building towards

CAC's mission is to help every student reach their fullest musical and artistic potential — whatever their age, ability or ambition.

  • —Educate complete musicians with a firm grasp of the classics alongside new and recent repertoire.
  • —Attract highly talented individuals and guide them toward their fullest musical potential.
  • —Serve as a resource for the wider community, with training suited to every age and ability.
  • —Keep investing in instruments and infrastructure that support and enhance every student's education.
  • —Follow the Trinity College of Music, London syllabus, so students' work connects to a wider world of knowledge.

Campus rules & regulations
  • Students should conduct themselves, within and outside the campus, in a manner befitting this institute.
  • Students are required to be dressed neatly and decently; use of cell phones is not permitted in classrooms.
  • Please show courtesy to administrators, teachers, staff and fellow students at all times.
  • Be punctual, and don't leave class without the teacher's permission.
  • Fees are due by the 10th of every month; unpaid fees may mean a trainee cannot attend class.
  • Carry your attendance / fees card to every class.
  • Instruments may not be played or removed from class without the instructor's permission — students who damage instruments are responsible for them.
  • This is an eco-friendly campus: please don't bring plastic or other waste inside.
